What was adapted
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The focus-guard feature ("Focus guard", 防沉迷) is a Kotlin re-implementation
based on the design and logic of anti-addiction's floating-mask mechanism:

  * the decision flow that reacts to foreground-package changes
  * the keyword matching direction (a page node's text is considered a hit
    when it is contained in one of the configured keywords)
  * the per-app quota model (daily free passes, temporary "leisure" periods)
  * the configurable mask offsets that keep the top and bottom edges of the
    screen clear so that search and navigation stay usable

The following parts of the original project were deliberately NOT ported:

  * all network functionality (device-information reporting and
    server-delivered configuration). ZakoCountdown's focus guard is
    fully offline and stores its configuration only on the device.
  * the MMKV storage dependency (replaced with SharedPreferences + Gson)
  * the multi-tier quota and cooldown ladder (reduced to two parameters:
    daily passes and minutes per unlock)
